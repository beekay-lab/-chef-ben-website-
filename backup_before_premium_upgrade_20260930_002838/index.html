PASSION BEHIND THE PLATE

Benson Kamau Wangui, professionally known as Chef Ben, is a professional chef and pastry specialist with 13 years of experience in the hospitality industry.

His professional experience includes Great Rift Valley Lodge, Amboseli Serena Safari Lodge and Sentrim Hotels.

Chef Ben specialises in professional cooking, catering, fine dining, African, Continental and Indian cuisine, with a particular focus on pastry and bakery.
